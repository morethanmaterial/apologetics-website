## The central claim

Christian apologetics is not merely about answering isolated objections. It is about presenting a better account of reality: one that can make sense of reason, morality, consciousness, beauty, history, and human longing.

A purely material view of the world often struggles to explain why these things matter deeply.

## A simple argument

1. Human beings experience reason, moral obligation, consciousness, and meaning.
2. These features of reality are difficult to reduce to matter alone.
3. Christianity offers a coherent explanation for why reality includes both the material and the immaterial.
4. Therefore, the Christian worldview deserves serious consideration.
